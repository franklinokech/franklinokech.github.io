Built a real-time Clinical Information Network Dashboard that enables clinicians and policymakers to monitor neonatal outcomes efficiently. The platform visualizes key metrics such as mortality rates, diagnosis distributions, hospital admissions, and discharge statistics, helping healthcare professionals make informed decisions and track trends across multiple facilities. 

The dashboard is designed for clarity, interactivity, and actionable insights, supporting evidence-based improvements in neonatal care.
